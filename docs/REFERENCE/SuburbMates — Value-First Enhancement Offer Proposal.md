# SuburbMates — Value-First Enhancement Offer Proposal

**Status:** Paused proposal. This document does not authorise billing, pricing, paid
placement, a public offer, entitlement records, or a Stripe integration. D-021 makes
evidence-limited official-website enrichment and outcome validation the prerequisite
to reopening commercial implementation.

## 1. Purpose

This proposal makes D-018's future commercial direction concrete enough to review
without weakening the free directory. The product is valuable only when a local
visitor can find an honest profile, understand what a business does, and contact it
directly. An owner should never need to pay for publication, ownership, corrections,
direct contact details, factual accuracy, review, or ordinary organic discovery.

## 2. Free directory baseline

The following remain free and are never commercial entitlements:

- publication when a business deterministically qualifies under an approved source
  contract;
- claiming a business and proposing factual corrections, description, hours and
  owner-authorised media for review;
- direct phone, email, website and directions actions;
- organic directory search and its relevance ordering;
- source attribution, owner control, moderation, privacy and audit history.

## 3. Proposed future offer

The first offer, if approved, should be an **owner-chosen Enhanced local profile**.
It is a presentation and participation product, not a trust badge, search boost or
publication condition.

Potential included value, subject to a separate approved specification:

- a clearly labelled, owner-authored profile story or local-services section;
- additional owner-authorised profile media beyond the ordinary free profile need;
- an optional, plainly labelled local spotlight module outside organic search
  results, with an identical editorial-style card treatment for every participant;
- aggregate first-party outcome reporting for that owner: profile views and direct
  contact actions only, never visitor identities, search terms or cross-site data.

No feature may imply endorsement, verification, superior service quality, or a
recommendation by SuburbMates. A paid participant must remain visually distinct from
organic results wherever the placement is not part of their own profile.

## 4. Non-negotiable commercial boundary

Payment must not affect:

- whether a business is listed, claimed, published, corrected or retained;
- organic search relevance, typo handling, category matching or result order;
- a business's factual trust signals, source evidence, verification language or
  moderation outcome;
- access to its own request history, owner dashboard or direct contact routes.

The operator must not have to process routine sales work in `/ops`. A future
commercial lifecycle must be self-service where safe, with only genuine payment or
policy exceptions becoming protected, auditable operator work.

## 5. Required approval before any implementation

The owner must approve all of the following as one commercial specification:

1. the exact customer-facing benefit and any limits;
2. pricing, billing period, taxes and any introductory offer;
3. eligibility and owner-authorisation requirements;
4. the presentation locations and explicit paid-placement labels;
5. entitlement start, renewal, cancellation, refund, payment-failure and expiry
   behaviour;
6. Stripe product/price identifiers, verified webhook model, idempotency and
   reconciliation responsibilities;
7. the data-retention, privacy and aggregate reporting rules;
8. controlled acceptance, rollback and a real-production observation plan.

Until that approval and D-021's enrichment/outcome evidence, the price is **unset**,
Stripe stays disabled, and there is no public CTA or implied upgrade path.

## 6. Future implementation gates

After approval, implementation must be split and independently verified:

1. owner-visible offer terms and an explicit consent/checkout boundary;
2. server-authorised entitlement records that cannot change publication, ownership
   or organic ranking;
3. Stripe checkout and signed, idempotent webhook processing;
4. cancellation/failure expiry that removes only commercial presentation while
   preserving the free directory profile;
5. aggregate, first-party owner outcome reporting with no visitor profiling;
6. protected exception visibility in `/ops`, immutable audit events and controlled
   acceptance of success, failure, recovery and cancellation paths.

## 7. Current state

No part of this proposal is active. Existing owner-profile readiness, media review,
direct-contact controls and website enrichment are free directory capabilities, not a
paid tier. D-004, D-018 and D-021 remain the controlling authority for any later
commercial decision.
