# SuburbMates — Future Owner Enhancement Offer Brief

## Status

**Proposal only — not approved for sale or implementation.**

This brief turns D-018's value-first commercial direction into a decision-ready offer shape. It does not authorise pricing, Stripe, subscriptions, entitlements, advertising placement, public labels, database changes, emails, or operator work. The free directory remains the product currently available to every legitimate local business.

## Product promise

An owner may eventually choose extra presentation and promotion tools **after** a useful free profile exists. They pay for optional business expression and clearly-labelled reach, never for legitimacy, a claim outcome, factual trust, inclusion, or better organic search rank.

## Candidate offer shape

| Layer | Owner receives | Does not receive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free directory profile | A published eligible profile; direct contact paths; claim, correction, owner-story, hours and approved media workflows; ordinary organic discovery. | A promise of traffic, a trust badge, paid rank, or preferential publication. |
| Future enhanced profile | A guided owner-content review, richer approved gallery/logo/story/service presentation, and optional seasonal information blocks. | Unreviewed copy or media, invented facts, a `Verified` implication, publication, or organic-rank movement. |
| Future clearly labelled local promotion | A time-bounded placement outside organic results, such as a separately-labelled local spotlight module, only where it is relevant to the page context. | Insertion into organic results, a claim of recommendation, a trust signal, cross-site targeting, lead resale, or access to personal visitor data. |

## Non-negotiable terms

1. **Organic results stay neutral.** Search, taxonomy and ordinary directory order never read a payment or tier field.
2. **Free remains useful.** Core profile facts, direct-contact actions, claim, corrections and owner-approved factual improvements cannot be withheld to force payment.
3. **No false authority.** A paid label must identify a commercial promotion accurately; it cannot say or imply `verified`, `recommended`, `best`, `trusted`, `official`, or similar.
4. **Owner control and review.** Every owner-supplied fact, image, logo, offer or seasonal message follows the existing permission, moderation and immutable-audit rules. The owner can remove their optional material; revocation is not hidden behind a billing path.
5. **Privacy and direct contact.** No lead resale, advertising pixel, cross-site tracking, visitor profile, search-term retention or contact-message disclosure is part of the offer. Aggregate first-party observability remains separate from owner data.
6. **Clear separation.** Payment never creates a business, publishes/unpublishes it, grants/revokes ownership, resolves a claim, changes ABN/source evidence, or changes SEO eligibility.

## Required owner decisions before any implementation

The owner must explicitly approve all of the following in a dated decision record before a schema, Stripe, UI, email, entitlement or promotional-placement change is made:

1. One initial offer (enhanced profile, labelled local promotion, or both) and its exact public wording.
2. Price, billing period, taxes, trial/introductory terms and any geographic or category limits.
3. The exact entitlement inventory, including whether approved owner content remains visible after cancellation.
4. Placement inventory and label treatment, with evidence that it remains separate from organic results.
5. Purchase, renewal, payment failure, grace period, cancellation, refund and chargeback rules.
6. Reconciliation owner, evidence, support route, retention and deletion rules.
7. Success measures that use aggregate first-party data only, and the criteria for stopping the offer if it harms the free directory experience.

## Implementation gate

Only after those decisions are approved should a separate issue implement an entitlement model, Stripe integration, webhook/reconciliation lifecycle, protected operator surface, owner journey, cancellation flow, disclosures, tests and controlled acceptance. That work must prove that all free-directory, source, claim, media, privacy, audit and non-commercial ranking invariants remain intact.
